Can you donate blood?

You can usually donate if you meet these conditions. The final decision is always made by the doctor at the hospital or blood bank.

  • Aged between 18 and 60
  • Weigh at least 50 kg
  • In good health, with no fever or infection
  • At least 3 months since your last donation (4 months for women)

One donation can help save up to three lives.

Donated blood can be separated into components, so a single donation can help more than one patient.

Donation tips

What to do before, during and after you give blood

Before donation

  • Get a full night of sleep (7 to 8 hours).
  • Drink plenty of water in the hours before you donate.
  • Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal 2 to 3 hours earlier. Avoid very fatty food.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before.
  • Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily, and carry a photo ID.
  • Skip donating if you have a fever, cold or feel unwell.

During donation

  • A short health check comes first: your pulse, blood pressure and haemoglobin.
  • The blood draw itself takes about 10 minutes. The whole visit is usually under an hour.
  • Sit back, breathe slowly and keep your arm still.
  • A new sterile needle is used for every donor.
  • Tell the staff right away if you feel dizzy, cold or unwell.

After donation

  • Rest for 10 to 15 minutes and have a snack and a drink before you leave.
  • Drink extra fluids for the next 24 to 48 hours.
  • Avoid heavy lifting and hard exercise for the rest of the day.
  • Keep the bandage on for a few hours. If you feel dizzy, lie down with your feet raised.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours.
  • Update your last donation date in your BloodCircle profile.

This is general guidance. Follow the instructions given by the doctor or staff at your blood bank.

Frequently asked questions

Will I feel weak after donating? +

A standard donation is safe for healthy adults. Your body replaces the fluid within hours. Drink plenty of water and eat well before and after donating.

Why does my profile show "Unavailable" after I donate? +

Your body needs time to recover. After your last donation you are unavailable for 3 months (4 months for women). When that time ends, you become available again automatically.

Can everyone see my phone number? +

No. Donor phone numbers are only visible to logged-in users, which helps reduce spam.

What should I do if I need blood? +

Log in and click "Request blood", then enter the patient's details. Your request will appear on the home page. You can also open the Volunteer page and call donors in your area directly.
